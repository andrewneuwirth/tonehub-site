How Match works

From the record to your amp.

No EQ curves. No guesswork. Just the knobs on your amp.

  1. Play the song

    Tap What's playing? and let your iPhone listen, or pick an audio file.

  2. Pick the part

    Tell ToneHub which part you're after: the main riff, a verse, the solo.

  3. Get your settings

    ToneHub translates the tone to the amp you own, in its own knobs.

    Gain 6.5Bass 6Mid 4.5Treble 6

  4. Hear it, then play it

    Preview the tone with Hear it, try it on ToneHub's amp, and save it for later.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is ToneHub free?

Yes. ToneHub is free, with no ads.

When can I get it?

ToneHub is coming soon to iPhone, with a Mac version later. Email us and we'll let you know when it's out.

Do I need an audio interface?

Only to play your guitar through ToneHub's amp. Match, the tuner and the other practice tools work with your iPhone's microphone.

Does Match work with my amp?

Pick your amp and ToneHub gives you settings in its own knobs. No amp handy? Use ToneHub's built-in amp.

Does ToneHub record me or upload my audio?

No. Your guitar and the songs you match are processed on your iPhone. A clip you match stays in memory while its result is open, and is never saved or uploaded. See the Privacy Policy.

Do I need an account?

No. An account is optional and only needed to share tones with other players.

Where do the videos come from?

Videos play in YouTube's official player, with YouTube's controls. ToneHub never downloads or changes them.
