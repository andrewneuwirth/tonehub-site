FAQ

Does Match work with my amp?

Yes. Pick your amp and you get settings in its own knobs.

Do I need an audio interface?

Only to play through ToneHub's amp. Everything else uses your iPhone's mic.

Is my audio uploaded?

No. Audio stays on your iPhone. See the Privacy Policy.
