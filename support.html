Reporting a problem

It helps to include:

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version
  • Your audio interface, if you use one
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if you can

Common requests

No sound from the amp

Monitoring is off at launch so the app never feeds back. On Play, connect your interface and turn on Monitor. Use headphones or an interface, not the built-in mic and speaker.

Microphone access

Match and the tuner need the microphone. Turn it on in the iOS Settings app › ToneHub.

Delete your account

In the app, go to Settings › Account › Delete account. This removes your account, profile and the tones you published.

Privacy and data requests

Ask us to delete any data related to you at support@tonehub.app. See the Privacy Policy.
